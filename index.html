National Water Meter Rollout — Firmware Defect Caught in Pilot

DataSoft Systems · Department of Telecommunications

QA CATCH

During pilot testing of a national smart water meter rollout, I tested devices across the full operating range rather than only typical use. That surfaced a defect in the pulse-counting logic: above 15 L/min, the firmware under-reported flow by roughly 8%, which would have produced systematic billing errors across every installed unit.

The fix was three lines of firmware. Finding it before production deployment prevented a full post-rollout recall. It's the clearest example I have of why boundary testing matters more than happy-path testing.
